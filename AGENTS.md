# Working on Cargo-Grid

- Work only in this project unless the user explicitly authorizes another checkout. Preserve existing work, the MIT notice and the independently authored generator. Do not vendor workbench code, private references, slicer profiles or restricted third-party meshes; local reference inspection does not grant redistribution or design rights.
- Python source and named parameters own design intent. STEP, STL, 3MF, PNG and viewer data are derived. Keep ordinary generated work under ignored output directories; only intentionally documented, source-derived overviews and per-variant thumbnails under `docs/images/` belong in source control.
- Preserve default compatibility invariants: millimeters, southwest body-corner origin, Z=0 underside, 60 mm pitch, 13 mm reference height and original roofed joints. Full-height joints are explicitly experimental with the documented thin-web limitation. Do not silently alter interfaces, scaling or fit allowances.
- Holes and roof support are off by default. Interior hole scope is the default when holes are enabled; full scope is explicit. Enabled roof support targets retained west/south female roofs, with critical coverage and an intentional PETG-base/PLA-interface zero-contact workflow: top gap 0, independent support layers off, dense spacing 0, at least two top interface layers and build-plate-only off. Preserve all five process markers. Reject unsuitable/same-material roof requests; do not infer chemical compatibility from display names.
- Every generated Bambu plate defaults to automatic Convenience Mode (`Auto For Match`) without physical map caches. Custom mapping is explicit only. Initial support-foot expansion stays native automatic unless requested otherwise. The zero-contact PETG/PLA workflow preserves its measured 0.40 mm XY side-clearance safeguard; do not apply it to unrelated workflows or change bridge, threshold, cooling, speed or prime/flush settings to hide contact problems.
- H2D dual-safe catalogues use common reach X25..325, Y0..320, Z<=320, a further 5 mm model inset and 10 mm model gaps, with family-coherent named plates. Keep the 5x5 tile isolated and explicitly left-nozzle-only; do not scale it or let automatic mapping span both exclusive zones. Validate support/brim/tower bounds separately from visible model bounds.
- Full-hole 2x1 roof-support jobs intentionally occupy the three female-edge round cutouts at (0,30), (30,0) and (90,0) with removable support during printing. Explain removal access; do not claim these holes are open during printing or permanently blocked after removal. Keep protected X openings clear.
- Preserve CLI/API names, manifest semantics, quantities, overwrite refusal and unsupported-combination errors. Roof-plus-stack and mixed-catalogue roof support remain unsupported. A documentation change must not change geometry or public behavior.
- The unreleased `lock-90` family is replaced by `vertical-tile-bracket`; do not restore an alias or obsolete upright walls. Keep the original floor/wall 1x2, 2x1 and 2x2 variants plus only the approved shallow floor 1x1 -> wall 1x2 and floor 2x1 -> wall 2x2 variants. `nx` is base/wall width, `ny` is floor depth and optional `panel_height_cells` is wall height; omit/match it for stable original identities. Preserve panel seat Y47, tips Y59.8, filled backing over connector roots, mixed R2 free-body/R1 bearing-lip rounds, the internal bearing corner, exact X regions, separate ordinary tiles and nominal zero-gap strip. Original brackets use their computed retangent rear-face-down pose; shallow Bambu exports are Y=-90 side-down with object-scoped normal Auto. Support reaches both shallow X mating regions and requires removal/fit review.
- Keep `ramp` as the approved floor-to-mat transition at 13 mm rise, 50 mm finished run and integer 60 mm width cells, with one unchanged original roofed female tile-edge pocket per cell. It extends in positive Y from a north male tile edge; do not imply a 90-degree rotation changes joining sex/direction. Preserve R2 free exterior edges and the original mating boundary. Full-height/custom-interface ramps, corner ramps, male adapters, hole patterns and copied third-party contours remain unsupported.
- Keep the eight `vertical-stop` variants at explicit 1x1, 1x2, 2x1 and 2x2 bases crossed with H60/H120 shoulder heights. They are full-width filled wedges with no wall holes, open ribs, panel plugs or ledges; 2x1/H120 uses the natural wedge, not a raised 45-degree toe. Preserve the coupled R2 blend on every free exterior edge and exact X plugs/roots. Object-scoped normal Auto is intentional; 1x1/H120 and 2x1/H120 support can enter the mounting region and require removal/fit review.
- Keep both `lock-45` variants as full-width filled wedges, not the earlier open-backed wall/web construction. Preserve the 45-degree cargo face, 4.1 mm toe, 6 mm horizontal cap, R2 coupled across all 18 free body edges, exact X connectors/roots, unchanged outer bounds and X=-135-degree Bambu pose. Do not add object-scoped support; the bounded native OFF/Auto checks were support-free.
- Bambu exports apply plate X=180 body-down, each original bracket's computed retangent rear-face-down X rotation, shallow-bracket Y=-90, per-design normal-stop broad-rear-face-down and angled-stop X=-135 before fit checks and packing; source STEP/STL/core 3MF retain model orientation. Record exact transforms and artifact-specific applied flags.
- Use R3 on original-style edge/corner bodies and straight rail/support bodies, R3/R2 on straight rail windows, R2 on plate and angled-stop bodies, mixed R2/R1 on brackets, and R2 on normal stops/ramps. Rail ends use their tested per-variant body/window/rim radii from `accessories.py`; do not replace them with one blanket value. Apply exact tile/support dovetails and X connectors after rounding their nonfunctional bodies. Keep experimental full-height rounding separate. Do not relax geometry or mesh tolerances to accommodate kernel failures.
- CLI names encode units and meaning: `--build-width-mm`, `--build-depth-mm`, `--build-height-mm`, `--tile-height-mm`, `--stop-height-mm`, named `*-mm` gaps/diameters, and explicit `--width-cells`/`--depth-cells`/`--length-cells` counts. Keep ramp callers on `--width-cells` only because its run is fixed. `--copy-count` repeats one design; layout width/depth describe the whole assembly. Do not restore positional `--cells`/`--footprint` or the old ambiguous options; manifest/API fields remain stable.
- Read the relevant source/tests before editing. Validate generated geometry and exports, not just constants: body validity/count, positive volume, actual dimensions, interfaces, holes, layout and mesh topology. Do not relax tolerances, mesh-repair budgets or expected results merely to make a check pass.
- Use `pyproject.toml` and normal `uv sync` with configured feeds. Keep `uv.lock`, environments, caches and runtime data ignored; do not add machine-specific registry URLs. Documentation-only rendering must not add runtime dependencies.
- Use relative repository paths in maintained docs/examples. Keep prose paragraphs and list items on one source line. Keep guidance evergreen: no session histories, one-off success counts, private paths or links to ignored study artifacts.
- Run the smallest relevant tests first, plus `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `git diff --check`. On the documented workstation, run the portable suite with `uv run pytest -q -n 4 --dist loadfile -m "not native and not reference"`; use fewer workers on smaller machines. Keep native Bambu and local-reference checks serial with `uv run pytest -q` and set `CARGO_GRID_BAMBU`/`CARGO_GRID_REFERENCE` only for resources supplied for that run. Unavailable external checks remain skips, not passes.
- For packaging changes run `uv build` and `uv run python tools/check_distributions.py dist` in a fresh build directory. Verify a clean wheel install separately from an editable checkout. Prepare authorized source releases from reviewed tracked files, not a working-directory ZIP; see `docs/release-checklist.md`.
- When external CAD tools are requested, use the approved current workbench interpreter from this design root, generate an explicit project-relative STEP and inspect that same STEP. Prefer an actually available maintained CAD viewer; otherwise report the capability gap and use its documented fallback. Do not mix viewer reference systems or substitute a demo model.
- Render documentation from committed generator APIs, record the source/tool revisions and exact inventory scope, and check coverage, labels, links and image size. Do not imply exhaustive coverage of unbounded parametric dimensions.
- Core 3MF is geometry; native-compatible 3MF contains diagnostic, unsliced project metadata, not calibrated factory profiles or print approval. GUI actions, printer operations, package/model publication, commits and pushes require explicit authorization.
- Keep geometric checks, actual slicer observations and physical results separate. Fit, support removal, flatness, strength and service suitability remain unverified until applicable observations are supplied and recorded. Never infer physical outcomes from silence or a valid solid.
